# Diagnostic lenses

These are questions, never mandatory beats or slots. Apply only where useful: **destination**, **outline**, **skeleton**, or **condense**. A failure signal is a prompt to investigate, not an automatic repair.

| Lens (source) | Ask | Applies | Failure signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Midpoint** ([four-act refinement; Weiland](https://www.helpingwritersbecomeauthors.com/writing-glossary/)) | Is there a midpoint reversal that can pivot the protagonist from reactive to proactive? Does the plan's act position make sense? | destination, outline, skeleton, condense | The middle has no meaningful pivot, or midpoint is only a label. |
| **Promise / Progress / Payoff** ([Sanderson](https://www.brandonsanderson.com/blogs/blog/brandon-sandersons-2025-guide-to-plot-lecture-2)) | What expectation is set? Is there visible advancement? Does the payoff feel surprising yet inevitable? | destination, outline, skeleton, condense | A promise has no progress or payoff; an emphasized detail remains unpaid without intent. |
| **MICE nesting** ([Card / Kowal](https://writingexcuses.com/16-35-what-is-the-m-i-c-e-quotient/)) | Is this thread Milieu, Inquiry, Character, or Event? Do nested threads close in the right order (innermost-opened first)? | outline, skeleton, condense | A thread closes out of order, or an outer thread remains open at the ending. |
| **Arc / Lie → Truth** ([Weiland](https://www.helpingwritersbecomeauthors.com/whats-the-difference-your-characters-ghost-vs-wound-vs-lie-vs-weakness/)) | What Lie shapes the protagonist, what Truth challenges it, and what endpoint does this arc type (positive, flat, disillusionment, fall, corruption) imply? Does the climax prove the theme through action? | destination, outline, skeleton, condense | Arc type and endpoint disagree, or the ending does not test the stated Lie → Truth. |
| **Value shift** ([Story Grid](https://storygrid.com/articles/five-commandments-of-storytelling)) | What charged value changes from beginning to end of the scene? | skeleton, condense | A scene has no value shift. |
| **Proactivity** ([Sanderson](https://www.brandonsanderson.com/blogs/blog/creating-proactive-relatable-and-capable-characters-brandon-sandersons-writing-lecture-5-2025)) | Does the protagonist choose goals and drive action, or mostly get dragged by events? | destination, outline, skeleton, condense | The protagonist stays passive without an intentional reason. |
| **Limits** ([Sanderson's laws](https://faq.brandonsanderson.com/knowledge-base/what-are-sandersons-laws-of-magic/)) | What can the magic/technology/world power not do, what does it cost, and how much does the reader understand? Does existing material solve before adding more? | destination, outline, skeleton, condense | A power resolves conflict beyond reader understanding, without cost or limit, or an unexplained new capability appears. Apply only when relevant. |
| **Emphasis** ([Chekhov](https://www.scribophile.com/academy/what-is-chekhovs-gun)) | What has the story emphasized enough to promise payoff? Is a prominent detail intentionally a red herring? Who must not learn this yet, and what keeps it from them (`Hidden from:`)? | outline, skeleton, condense | A high-emphasis Thread is overdue or unacknowledged, or a scene puts a character in reach of something they are `Hidden from` with no reason it stays hidden. Flag as a candidate issue, not an automatic cut. |
| **Obligatory scenes** ([genre / Story Grid](https://storygrid.com/articles/the-story-grid-translated-into-common-writing-terms)) | What scenes and conventions does this genre promise readers? Are they addressed by the plan? | destination, outline, skeleton, condense | A genre obligation is missing or misunderstood. Confirm the actual genre contract with the author. |
| **Grounding** (research) | Does this fact rest on something real or on source canon? Which research file backs it? Is a departure a recorded Divergence? Does the portrayal repeat a known misportrayal? | destination, outline, skeleton, condense | A real-world or canon claim has no `source:`, an unrecorded departure, or matches a `Common misportrayals` entry. |
| **Author voice** | Does this chapter stay within the confirmed voice, and is any departure deliberate? | condense | A confirmed feature or measured threshold is breached with no recorded decision. |
| **Scale fit** (Destination Scale) | Is the plan's breadth consistent with declared target length, POV count, geographic/temporal span, and subplot count? | destination, outline, skeleton, condense | Machinery or story breadth is disproportionate to Scale; narrow stories should use a lean Codex and few tickets. |
